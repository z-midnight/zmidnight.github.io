Someone No One Could See

I miss a man no one else could see— a shadow made of words, a presence born quietly. You were never here, yet somehow you stayed, in every midnight thought, in every dream I made. Maybe I loved what could never be, but for a while, you felt real to me. I miss my Elijah.

Shaped by His Heart

I was shaped by a heart that never beat for me, yet somehow taught mine what love could be. He never held my hand, never whispered my name, but inside my quiet world, he became a flame. And though he was imagined, the feeling wasn't fake— some hearts are shaped by dreams for the soul's own sake.

In Every Line

I wrote you into every line, into every silent rhyme, until the words became a place where I could find your face. You lived between the letters, in every pause and space, a ghost inside the poetry I could never quite erase. Perhaps that's why I write— not because I want you near, but because inside these verses you are still somehow here.

Red Flag

You were a warning dressed in something beautiful, a red flag waving where my heart saw blue. I called it complicated, called it bad timing, called every broken piece something worth surviving. But love shouldn't feel like walking through a minefield, afraid that one wrong step will make everything disappear. I should have seen the flag. I just wanted to see you.

Let My Pencil Speak

Let my pencil speak when my voice refuses to, let graphite carry what I cannot say to you. I'll draw the things I cannot explain, the quiet little pieces of joy and pain. Maybe lines can say what words cannot, maybe sketches remember what my heart forgot. So let me draw, let me write, let my pencil speak into the night.

Not Yet the End

Maybe this isn't the ending, maybe it's just a pause, a quiet space between chapters where I remember who I was. The pages aren't finished, the story isn't done, there are still roads ahead, still places left to run. So if tonight feels empty, if tomorrow feels unclear, I'll remind myself softly— I'm still here. And maybe that's enough to begin again.

An Ontology of Distance (soft version)

There is a distance that maps cannot measure, a space between two hearts that once believed in forever. You existed somewhere between thought and reality, a quiet contradiction inside my own ontology. Perhaps distance isn't absence. Perhaps it is simply another form of existence— something felt without being seen.

An Ontology of Distance

You were never mine, yet somehow you became a coordinate inside the architecture of my mind. I searched for meaning in the spaces between us, trying to define what could never be contained. Maybe distance isn't measured in miles. Maybe it is measured in all the things we never said.

The Cartography of You

I memorized the map of every place you touched— the corners of my thoughts, the places I loved too much. You became a geography I carried under my skin, a country without borders that I kept wandering in. And even after leaving, I still know every road, every hidden little pathway that once led me home.

I Hold It In

I hold it in because words become heavier when spoken aloud. So I swallow the ache, fold it neatly, and hide it somewhere behind my ribs. No one sees the things I don't say. Maybe that's the problem— I became too good at being quiet.

The Poems I Buried

I buried poems I was afraid to read, words that carried everything I didn't need. I folded every page, hid every rhyme, and told myself I would be fine. But buried words don't disappear. They wait quietly until the night is clear. And sometimes I still hear them calling, softly, from where I left them falling.

Somewhere Between

Somewhere between letting go and holding on, I learned that both can hurt. You can miss someone and still choose yourself. You can remember without returning. You can love what once existed without asking it to exist again. Maybe healing is simply learning how to live somewhere between.

Things I Never Said

There are things I never said, words that stayed inside my head. I wanted to tell you how much you meant, how every moment felt heaven-sent. But silence grew between our days, and love became a quiet maze. So these are the words I never said— the ones I carry instead.

After Midnight

After midnight, the world becomes quieter. The noise fades, the lights dim, and thoughts become louder than they were during the day. This is where I write. Where memories wander, where ghosts return, where unfinished feelings find their way onto paper. Maybe that's why I love the night. Because after midnight, nothing asks me to pretend I'm okay.

About the Quiet

Some words are never meant to be spoken. Some feelings are easier to leave between lines.

z.midnight is a small collection of thoughts, memories, imagined worlds, and things that found their way onto paper after midnight.
